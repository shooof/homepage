## About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Statistics at Pennsylvania State University. My research
lies at the intersection of statistical machine learning, trustworthy AI, and
biomedical data science. I develop statistically principled methods for
identifying LLM-generated content and study temporal and causal models for
longitudinal electronic health records.

## Research Interests

- **Trustworthy Language Models:** watermarking, detection, provenance, and responsible deployment
- **Statistical Machine Learning:** hypothesis testing, power analysis, optimization, and statistical modeling
- **Biomedical Data Science:** electronic health records, survival and recurrent-event modeling, disease-risk prediction, and causal inference

## Selected Research

### Power-Calibrated LLM Watermarking

I developed a statistical framework that connects logit-based watermark
parameters to detection power and KL distortion in closed form. The framework
turns parameter selection into a constrained optimization problem and
consistently identifies favorable Pareto operating points across language
models and datasets.

### Marked Hawkes Processes for Recurrent Events

I model longitudinal, multimodal EHR data using marked temporal point processes
to estimate disease risk and progression. This work includes data-processing
and real-world evaluation workflows for MIMIC-IV.
